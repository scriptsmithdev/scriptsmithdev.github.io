About Me

My name is Felix Amoah. I am a hardworking, adaptable and continuously learning professional with experience in security operations, warehouse management, construction and industrial work.

My professional journey has taken me through different industries and working environments in Ghana, the United Arab Emirates and Qatar. These experiences have helped me develop discipline, responsibility, teamwork, attention to detail and the ability to work effectively under pressure.

I began my professional journey as a Warehouse Man at Guinness Ghana Limited, Ahinsan. This experience strengthened my understanding of inventory handling, warehouse operations, organization, safety procedures and teamwork.

I have a professional experience in Ghana as a Security Receptionist at Oak Plaza, Asokwa, where I was responsible for maintaining a professional security presence while assisting with the safety and orderly operation of the facility.

I later gained international work experience in Dubai, working with City Nights Contracting LLC as security personnel. Working in a different country exposed me to new professional standards, multicultural teams and demanding working environments.

My industrial experience continued with Emkan for Engineering and Contracting Services, where I worked as a Assistant Pipe Fitter for sometimes and then transition to be a Rigger. This role introduced me to industrial construction environments, equipment handling, safety awareness and working according to established procedures.

Today, I am expanding my career into technology. I am currently enrolled in a six-month DevSecOps course and I am approaching the four-month mark of my training. My goal is to combine my practical work experience with cloud computing, automation, infrastructure, security and software delivery.

I believe that DevSecOps is not only about technology. It also requires discipline, responsibility, security awareness, continuous learning and the ability to solve problems systematically — qualities I have developed throughout my professional journey.
