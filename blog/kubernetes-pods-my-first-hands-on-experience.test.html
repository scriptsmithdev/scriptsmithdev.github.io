Kubernetes Pods: My First Hands-On Experience

Today I learned the fundamentals of Kubernetes Pods and how they are used to run containerized workloads.

What I Learned

I learned what a Kubernetes Pod is, why Pods are the smallest deployable unit in Kubernetes, and how containers run inside Pods.

What I Did

I created and worked with a Kubernetes Pod and practiced using kubectl to inspect and manage it.

The Problem

I had difficulty understanding the relationship between a Pod and the container running inside it.

How I Solved It

I reviewed the Pod configuration and used kubectl commands to inspect the Pod and understand its status and configuration.

Key Takeaways

Pods provide the execution environment for containers in Kubernetes. kubectl is essential for interacting with Kubernetes resources. Understanding Pods is an important foundation for learning deployments and services.
